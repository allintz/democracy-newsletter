# Bayesian Analysis: Likelihood of Compromising Video Evidence of Trump With Minors

*Published: February 20, 2026*

## Methodology and Disclaimers

This analysis applies Bayesian reasoning to publicly available evidence to estimate the probability that compromising video evidence exists showing Donald Trump engaging in sexual acts with minors. Bayesian analysis provides a structured framework for updating beliefs based on evidence, but the reader should understand several critical limitations:

1. **Prior selection is subjective.** There is no objective base rate for this kind of question. We present a range of priors and show how conclusions shift.
2. **Likelihood ratios are judgment calls.** Reasonable people can disagree on how strongly each piece of evidence supports or undermines the hypothesis.
3. **Evidence is not independent.** Many items below are correlated (e.g., flight logs and Ted Lieu's statements draw on the same underlying files). Naively multiplying likelihood ratios overstates certainty. We partially address this by grouping correlated evidence.
4. **This is an estimate of probability, not a determination of guilt.** Even a high posterior probability would not constitute proof. Trump has not been charged with any crime related to Epstein's victims and denies all wrongdoing.
5. **Allegations are not evidence.** We carefully distinguish between established facts, allegations, and speculation throughout.

---

## Hypothesis

**H:** Compromising video evidence exists—in the possession of any party—showing Trump engaging in sexual acts with minors.

This compound hypothesis requires three sub-conditions:

- **(A)** Trump engaged in sexual acts with minors at some point (primarily in connection with Epstein)
- **(B)** Such acts were captured on surveillance or other video recording
- **(C)** The recording still exists and has not been destroyed

**~H:** No such video exists (because A, B, or C is false).

## Part 2: Evidence Claims — Assessed Individually

### 2.1 Ted Lieu's Statements About the Unredacted Epstein Files

**What happened:** Rep. Ted Lieu (D-CA), who reviewed unredacted Epstein files, stated at a February 3, 2026 press conference that the files contain "highly disturbing allegations" of Trump "raping and threatening to kill children." He said Trump appears in the files "thousands and thousands of times." On February 11, he confronted AG Pam Bondi at a hearing, playing the 1992 Mar-a-Lago video and presenting an FBI tip in which a witness reported that a girl, later found dead, told him Trump and Epstein raped her. Lieu stated the DOJ never interviewed the witness. ([C-SPAN](https://www.c-span.org/clip/news-conference/rep-ted-lieu-claims-president-trump-is-accused-of-raping-children-in-the-epstein-files/5191713); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/11/politics/video/watch-exchange-between-lieu-and-bondi-over-trump-and-epstein-files-digvid-hrzt); [IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.com/democrat-given-access-unredacted-epstein-files-makes-bombshell-claim-about-trump-3797618); [Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/democratic-congressman-ted-lieu-demands-doj-interview-fbi-caller-who-made-rape-allegation-against-donald-trump/))

**Important context:** Fact-checkers have noted that Lieu referenced *allegations* in the files, not established facts or direct evidence. Many of the "thousands" of Trump references are incidental — flight logs, news clippings, third-party mentions. Lieu is a partisan Democrat with political incentives to maximize the damaging interpretation of ambiguous material. ([Lead Stories](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2026/02/fact-check-ted-lieu-did-not-say-epstein-files-show-trump-raping-children.html); [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/ted-lieu-makes-wild-claims-123705322.html))

**Assessment:** Lieu's statements are significant because he had access to unredacted files that the public has not seen. However, "allegations in files" is several steps removed from "evidence of guilt." The uninvestigated FBI tip is troubling — a competent investigation should have followed up on it — but a tip is not corroboration. **Moderately supportive of H, with significant uncertainty.**

### 2.2 The "Katie Johnson" / Jane Doe Lawsuit

**What happened:** In 2016, a woman using the pseudonym "Katie Johnson" filed suit in federal court alleging Trump and Epstein forcibly raped her at age 13 at Epstein's Manhattan townhouse in 1994 at underage sex parties. The suit included a corroborating declaration from "Tiffany Doe," who said she recruited the victim. The suit was filed, dismissed on procedural grounds, refiled in New York, and then withdrawn on November 4, 2016 — days before the election — after the plaintiff received threats. ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-katie-johnson-allegations-sexual-assault-case-dismissed-1921051); [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/news/2024/09/03/trump-epstein-katie-johnson/); [Tara Palmeri / Puck](https://www.tarapalmeri.com/p/the-disappearing-epstein-jane-doe))

**Credibility complications:** The initial filing was promoted by a publicist using the false name "Al Taylor," later identified as Norm Lubow, a former "Jerry Springer Show" producer. The first lawsuit included an unverifiable address and was filed without an attorney. ([Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/news/2024/09/03/trump-epstein-katie-johnson/))

**Credibility support:** Her later attorney confirmed she was a real person. Journalist Tara Palmeri reported in detail on the case. Michael Cohen, Trump's former fixer, told Palmeri that he worked on a similar Epstein-related case for Trump, with Trump dismissing it as "bullshit" and instructing Cohen to "take care of it." ([Tara Palmeri / Puck](https://www.tarapalmeri.com/p/exclusive-inside-the-trumpepstein))

**Assessment:** The Lubow involvement damages but does not destroy credibility. The threats, the Cohen angle, and the consistency with known facts about Epstein's Manhattan townhouse (where surveillance cameras were documented) lend some support. **Weakly to moderately supportive, with high uncertainty.**

### 2.3 Biden DOJ Did Not Prosecute

**What happened:** The Biden DOJ did not charge Trump in connection with Epstein's crimes. The Biden administration did not release the Epstein files, citing the ongoing investigation and DOJ independence norms. Harris defended this decision. ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/opinion/criminal-justice/5740973-why-biden-didnt-release-the-epstein-files-but-maybe-should-have/); [News Nation](https://www.newsnationnow.com/politics/why-were-epstein-files-not-released-biden/); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/8/6/fact-check-did-democrats-take-action-over-epstein-case-transparency))

**Assessment:** This is genuinely ambiguous evidence. It could mean:
- The evidence was insufficient (supports ~H)
- Institutional norms prevented action during an open investigation (neutral)
- Political calculations — Biden may have wanted to avoid the appearance of weaponizing DOJ against a political opponent (neutral to weakly supportive of H, since it explains inaction even if evidence existed)

A former federal prosecutor described the concept of "awful, but lawful" — where conduct is morally hideous but legally unprovable beyond reasonable doubt. This is not the same as exoneration.

However, the Trump DOJ's own July 2025 memo also concluded there was insufficient evidence — and the Trump DOJ has an obvious conflict of interest. Meanwhile, the Prince Andrew arrest, French and Latvian investigations all suggest both administrations may have been wrong or prematurely dismissive. **Weakly against H, but with substantial caveats.**

### 2.4 Iran Threatened to Release Compromising Material

**What happened:** A viral social media post (from @YourAnonTV on X) claimed: "BREAKING: Iranian hackers claim they now have videos of Trump with minors and will release them if the U.S. attacks Iran." ([Unilad](https://www.uniladtech.com/social-media/truth-viral-claim-hackers-threatening-videos-trump-632017-20260219))

**What actually happened:** This claim is **unsubstantiated and likely misinformation.** The real underlying events involve an Iran-linked hacking group (IRGC operatives later indicted by the DOJ) who stole emails from Trump campaign aides including Roger Stone and Susie Wiles. They threatened to release approximately 100GB of stolen *emails*, not videos. U.S. authorities called it a "calculated smear campaign" and "digital propaganda." Even X's own AI tool, Grok, noted the post appeared unsubstantiated. ([PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/watch-feds-unseal-criminal-charges-against-iranians-accused-of-hacking-trump-campaign); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/iran-hackers-threaten-leak-trump-emails-2092864); [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/us/articles/2025-07-01/us-calls-reported-threats-by-pro-iran-hackers-to-release-trump-tied-material-a-smear-campaign); [DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/three-irgc-cyber-actors-indicted-hack-and-leak-operation-designed-influence-2024-us))

**Assessment:** This should receive **essentially zero evidential weight.** The viral claim conflated stolen campaign emails with fabricated allegations about videos of minors. Including it in an analysis risks laundering misinformation. **No meaningful update.**

Steele's primary source later hedged to the FBI about the tape's existence. Steele himself told ABC News the tape "probably does" exist but wouldn't put "100% certainty on it." The Kremlin denies possessing kompromat. The Steele dossier's sourcing on this specific claim has been significantly damaged. **Weakly supportive of a general pattern of kompromat, but not directly relevant to the minors question. Very small update.**

However, it is undermined by: (a) the documented disappearance of evidence, (b) the conflict of interest of the issuing administration, (c) contradictory statements from the AG and FBI director, and (d) subsequent prosecutions abroad suggesting the "no evidence" assessment was incomplete. **Moderately against H, but with significant caveats.**

## Part 3: Bayesian Analysis

### Setting the Prior

The prior probability must be calibrated to the right reference class. We are not asking about a random person; we are asking about someone who:
- Maintained a close 15-year relationship with a convicted child sex trafficker
- Visited the trafficker's properties multiple times
- The trafficker operated hidden cameras at those properties
- Has been specifically accused (in a lawsuit) of participating in abuse

| Prior Stance | P(H) | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Skeptical | 3% | Most Epstein associates were not involved in crimes; video is a very specific claim |
| Moderate | 8% | Depth of relationship, documented behaviors, and surveillance infrastructure elevate probability |
| Credulous | 20% | Multiple red flags, length of association, and Epstein's documented modus operandi of using kompromat |

We use the **moderate prior of 8%** as our baseline, with sensitivity analysis below.

### Evidence Updates

We group correlated evidence to avoid overcounting.

#### Group 1: Depth of Trump-Epstein Relationship
*Evidence: 8+ flights, 15-year relationship, 1992 party video, "younger side" quote, Epstein's email about Trump knowing, Trump's admission about young women*

These are all facets of the same underlying reality: Trump and Epstein had an unusually close, long relationship during which Trump demonstrably knew about Epstein's interest in young women/girls.

- **P(this evidence pattern | H):** If Trump was involved in abuse, a deep, long relationship with Epstein with indicators of knowledge is highly expected. **~75%**
- **P(this evidence pattern | ~H):** Many wealthy men socialized with Epstein without participating in abuse. However, the *depth* of this relationship — the "younger side" quote, the acknowledgment about young women, Epstein's email — goes beyond casual association. **~25%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 3.0**

#### Group 2: Specific Allegations in the Files (Ted Lieu / FBI Tip)
*Evidence: Lieu's statements about allegations of rape and murder threats in unredacted files; FBI tip about a witness; DOJ failure to investigate*

- **P(this evidence | H):** If the hypothesis is true, you'd expect the files of a meticulous documentarian like Epstein to contain allegations and tips consistent with it. **~65%**
- **P(this evidence | ~H):** False or confused allegations do appear in large investigative files. Epstein's files span decades and millions of pages. A partisan lawmaker could emphasize the most alarming interpretations. However, the specific FBI tip about an uninvestigated witness is harder to dismiss. **~25%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 2.6**

#### Group 3: Katie Johnson / Jane Doe Lawsuit
*Evidence: Lawsuit alleging rape at age 13 at Epstein's Manhattan townhouse; corroborating declaration; threats; Michael Cohen's involvement in a similar case*

- **P(this evidence | H):** A victim filing suit is expected if abuse occurred. The specific details (Epstein's Manhattan townhouse, where cameras existed) and the Cohen angle add credibility. **~55%**
- **P(this evidence | ~H):** False accusations against public figures happen, and the Lubow involvement is a genuine red flag. However, the case was not conclusively debunked — it was withdrawn under alleged threats, and the victim never recanted. **~15%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 3.7**

#### Group 4: Surveillance Infrastructure + Missing Evidence
*Evidence: Documented cameras at Epstein's properties; FBI seizure of recording equipment; evidence that went missing from the safe; disconnected cameras in Palm Beach*

- **P(this evidence | H):** If compromising video was recorded, Epstein (who was "meticulous about documentation") would have stored it. The evidence going missing is consistent with deliberate destruction or concealment of kompromat. **~60%**
- **P(this evidence | ~H):** Epstein had surveillance regardless of Trump's involvement. Evidence could go missing for many reasons. **~40%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 1.5**

#### Group 5: Biden DOJ Inaction + DOJ "No Evidence" Memo
*Evidence: Neither Biden nor Trump DOJ prosecuted; DOJ memo says seized videos don't show abuse or implicate others*

- **P(this evidence | H):** If video evidence exists but went missing (Group 4), or is held by parties unknown to the DOJ, or the DOJ chose not to pursue for institutional/political reasons, this outcome is possible. **~30%**
- **P(this evidence | ~H):** If no evidence exists, the DOJ finding nothing and not prosecuting is highly expected. **~85%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 0.35** (evidence *against* H)

#### Group 6: Kompromat Claims (Steele Dossier / Mussayev / Ukraine)
*Evidence: Steele dossier Ritz-Carlton claims; Mussayev's statements about Russia and Kazakhstan having video; wartime context*

- **P(this evidence | H):** These claims are about adult encounters, not minors. Only tangentially relevant. If the general pattern of kompromat is real, it slightly increases plausibility that similar recordings from Epstein's cameras exist. **~35%**
- **P(this evidence | ~H):** These claims could be strategic disinformation by wartime actors. The Steele dossier's sourcing on this specific claim has been damaged. **~25%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 1.4**

#### Group 7: Iran "Threat"
*Evidence: Viral social media claim about Iranian hackers having videos of Trump with minors*

This is unsubstantiated misinformation conflating real email hacks with fabricated video claims.

- **Likelihood ratio: 1.0** (no update)

#### Group 8: Prince Andrew Arrest + International Investigations
*Evidence: Andrew arrested Feb. 19, 2026; France and Latvia opened investigations; contradicts DOJ's "no one else to charge" assessment*

- **P(this evidence | H):** If the files contain genuinely incriminating material on powerful people, prosecutions of *some* associates are expected. Andrew's arrest validates that the "no evidence" framing was wrong for at least one major figure. **~60%**
- **P(this evidence | ~H):** Andrew's case may be entirely separate from Trump's situation. Other associates' guilt doesn't imply Trump's. **~45%**
- **Likelihood ratio: 1.3**

### Calculating the Posterior

Starting with moderate prior odds of 8% (odds = 0.087):

| Evidence Group | Likelihood Ratio | Cumulative Odds |
|---|---|---|
| Prior | — | 0.087 |
| Group 1: Relationship depth | 3.0 | 0.261 |
| Group 2: Lieu / FBI tip | 2.6 | 0.679 |
| Group 3: Katie Johnson | 3.7 | 2.511 |
| Group 4: Missing surveillance | 1.5 | 3.767 |
| Group 5: DOJ inaction / no evidence | 0.35 | 1.318 |
| Group 6: Kompromat claims | 1.4 | 1.846 |
| Group 7: Iran claim | 1.0 | 1.846 |
| Group 8: Andrew arrest | 1.3 | 2.399 |

**Posterior odds: 2.40**
**Posterior probability: 2.40 / 3.40 = ~71%**

### Sensitivity Analysis

The result is highly sensitive to prior selection and likelihood ratios. Here we show how the posterior changes under different assumptions:

| Scenario | Prior | Adjustments | Posterior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skeptical** | 3% | All likelihood ratios reduced by 30% | ~33% |
| **Moderate** (baseline) | 8% | As calculated above | ~71% |
| **Accounting for correlation** | 8% | All ratios square-rooted to penalize non-independence | ~38% |
| **Credulous** | 20% | As calculated above | ~86% |
| **Heavily weight DOJ memo** | 8% | Group 5 ratio set to 0.15 (strong counter-evidence) | ~52% |

**Summary range: ~33% to ~86%, with a central estimate around 38-52% when properly accounting for evidence correlation and DOJ counter-evidence.**

## Part 4: Key Uncertainties

Several factors could dramatically shift this estimate in either direction:

### Factors that would increase the probability:
- Release of the full unredacted Epstein files to the public
- The missing safe contents or disconnected camera recordings surfacing
- Additional victims coming forward with corroborating testimony
- Foreign intelligence services releasing material they claim to possess
- DOJ witnesses from Lieu's highlighted FBI tip being interviewed

### Factors that would decrease the probability:
- Full transparent review of all seized digital media by an independent body confirming nothing incriminating
- The Katie Johnson accuser publicly recanting
- Multiple Epstein victims with direct knowledge affirmatively clearing Trump (note: Virginia Giuffre reportedly did state Trump was not involved, though the full context of that statement is debated)
- Foreign governments confirming their kompromat claims were fabricated

## Part 5: Conclusion

The publicly available evidence paints a deeply troubling picture of Trump's relationship with Epstein, but falls short of establishing that compromising video evidence of sexual acts with minors exists. The analysis suggests:

- **The claim that such video exists is not baseless.** The documented depth of the Trump-Epstein relationship, the existence and disappearance of Epstein's surveillance recordings, the specific allegations in the Katie Johnson lawsuit, and the disturbing (if unverified) allegations highlighted by Rep. Lieu all provide a non-trivial evidential foundation.

- **The claim is also far from proven.** The DOJ's assessment (across two administrations) found no incriminating video in seized materials. Key evidence went missing before it could be analyzed. The strongest specific allegation (Katie Johnson) has significant credibility problems. The Iran "threat" is misinformation. The kompromat claims involve adults, not minors.

- **The central adjusted estimate of ~33-52%** reflects genuine, irreducible uncertainty. The missing surveillance evidence is the critical unknown: if it was destroyed, the question may never be answered; if it exists in the possession of unknown parties, it could surface at any time.

- **Institutional failure is clear regardless.** Whether or not video evidence exists, the DOJ's failure to interview the FBI tipster Lieu highlighted, the disappearance of evidence from Epstein's safe, the destruction of jail surveillance footage, and the premature "no one else to charge" assessment (now contradicted by Prince Andrew's arrest) represent serious failures of accountability.

The most honest answer is: **we don't know, and the institutions that should have found out failed to do their jobs.**
